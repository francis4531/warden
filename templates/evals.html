Know whether an agent works, on your terms.

Agents are probabilistic, so Warden measures rates, not pass/fail. Annotate what goes wrong in real conversations, turn those into eval cases, score them with cheap code checks first and a judge only where judgment is needed, then change one thing and compare against the baseline. Eval runs never execute gated actions.
